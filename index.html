Nebula Xplorer Satellite

Nebula Xplorer is a cutting-edge X-ray satellite exploring extreme objects like black holes and neutron stars. By tracking how X-ray emissions change over time, it reveals what’s happening in these intense environments. This student-driven mission, developed with academic and industry partners, combines innovation with real-world expertise. With high time resolution and continuous observations, it uncovers how matter behaves, how jets form, and how these systems evolve. Using advanced spectral-timing techniques, it provides a unique view into the structure and physics of the universe’s most extreme regions. Ready to explore the unknown?
